Your projects stay in your hands.

Your measurements

The calculator runs in your browser and does not itself upload your inputs or use cookies or local storage. Inputs stay in the current page and reset when you reload. Advertising scripts also run on the tool pages as described below.

Downloads and clipboard

Project notes are generated in your browser. Copy results writes the displayed measurements and calculations to your clipboard when you choose it. Check the note before sharing it. Printing uses your browser’s print dialog.

Advertising and analytics

The three calculator pages load a third-party HilltopAds banner script from shameful-farm.com. The first click on Save project note, Copy results or Print this plan also attempts to open a sponsored offer on bold-consequence.com in a new tab or window while continuing the original feature. This happens at most once per page visit and may be blocked by your browser. You can close the top banner for the current page visit; refreshing restores it. Advertising providers and their partners may process your IP address, browser and device details, referring page and interactions, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Third-party scripts run on the page and can access its content, measurements and URL. There is no video advertising integration, and Ahrefs Web Analytics has not been enabled. See the HilltopAds privacy policy for the provider’s practices.

Hosting and external links

Your hosting provider may log IP addresses, requested URLs and device information according to its policies. Links to references open third-party sites with their own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can use the tools without an account. Avoid putting personal information in your saved notes. Closing the page clears the current calculator inputs from the page; downloaded files and clipboard contents remain on your device until you remove them.
