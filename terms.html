A helping hand for your handmade.

Using the tools

Stitchloom is a free planning tool for crochet and knitting. Results depend on the measurements you enter and assume a consistent gauge. Work a washed and dried swatch, check calculations against your pattern, and measure your project as you go.

Scope of the calculations

Gauge and blanket calculators assume rectangular fabric worked in regular rows. They do not grade garments, estimate yarn, handle corner-to-corner construction, or add foundation and turning chains. Stitch repeats use at least one complete repeat. Blanket presets are editable examples and are not product or safety standards.

Shaping instructions

Shaping uses one increase per selected starting stitch or one decrease over two starting stitches. It makes a balanced distribution of plain stitches across a single row, including edges. It does not reserve selvedges, mirror garment shaping or determine safe fit. Knitting uses kfb and k2tog; crochet examples use US sc, 2 sc in one stitch, and sc2tog.

No guarantee of outcome

Yarn, tension, laundering, blocking and construction affect the result. Check the predicted dimensions and make an appropriate test piece before committing to a project. There is no guarantee of fit, material quantity or suitability for a particular use.
